# Curtis Kusi Boadum

I work across customer operations and software. I build tools that make it easier to investigate issues, document what happened, and hand work over when it needs a person.

## Selected work

- **[SDR Voice Studio](https://github.com/curtisboadum/sdr-voice-studio-demo)** — a local browser voice prototype with speech recognition, spoken responses, and interruption handling.
- **[AuditReel](https://github.com/curtisboadum/auditreel-demo)** — an offline evidence-to-storyboard demo that links findings to their sources and refuses to draft from stale or conflicting evidence.
- **[FlowAudit](https://github.com/curtisboadum/flowaudit-platform)** — tooling for mapping business processes and turning what you learn into automation plans. [Product site](https://flowaudit.co.uk)
- **[Brandsearch CLI](https://github.com/curtisboadum/brandsearch-cli)** — a command-line client with dry runs and explicit approval for metered requests.
- **[Video to Agent Engine](https://github.com/curtisboadum/video-to-agent-engine)** — an experimental pipeline for turning a reference video into a documented, testable workflow.

My background includes customer support and operations. I'm interested in how AI can help teams investigate cases, explain the next step clearly, and know when to bring in a human.
